# Payment artwork sources

- mada: https://moyasar.com/payment-methods/mada.svg
- Visa: https://moyasar.com/payment-methods/visa.svg
- Mastercard: https://moyasar.com/payment-methods/master-card.svg
- Apple Pay: original SVG from https://developer.apple.com/apple-pay/marketing/Apple-Pay-Mark.zip
- Apple Pay usage guide: https://developer.apple.com/apple-pay/marketing/

Artwork is served locally without modifying brand shapes or colors. These are payment-method marks, not payment buttons. Actual availability is controlled by the merchant's Moyasar hosted checkout configuration and the customer's device/card.
